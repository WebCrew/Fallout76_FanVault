# Guild leader guide

Guild management is planned, not functional in this demo. A leader may use a Net-Twin group/page as a community presence. Players only need their account and relevant gamertag, not their own group/page.

A group is the better-supported candidate for guild chat based on installed code. Full mapping and membership lifecycle remain to be implemented. Leader rights must be checked server-side and must not grant global FanVault administration.

Planned invitations use authorised Net-Twin gamertag search. Tags are self-reported; finding a player does not override their chat privacy. Ordinary guild management needs no API key. Future bots/developer apps will require their own limited access mechanism.
